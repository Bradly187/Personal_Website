Building ClinicAgent — AI scheduling infrastructure for small clinics

BT

ClinicAgent originated as a team capstone project (CSCI-440 Group 7) at Texas A&M-RELLIS. I realized the core orchestrator and scheduling engine had real commercial value, so I've since rebuilt the platform as a real multi-tenant SaaS product.

The live system at clinic.bradtarver.com runs on a single AWS EC2 demo box with a Blazor front-end, and a coordinator LLM that routes natural language requests to specialist agents. The MCP server, REST API, and in-app chat all expose the exact same domain logic.

My background is across the full stack — AI agents, cloud infrastructure, systems programming, and healthcare software. I'm AWS certified (Cloud Practitioner, AI Practitioner, Cloud Application Developer) and an IBM Qiskit Advocate, with additional AWS certifications in progress. I graduate with a B.S. in Computer Science from East Texas A&M University in December 2026.
